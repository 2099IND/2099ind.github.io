Help

Frequently asked questions

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Common questions about 2099 Industries, the current state of this website and how future orders will be reviewed.

What is 2099 Industries?

2099 Industries develops and operates businesses, services and digital ventures built for the modern marketplace. The company is organized around distinct areas of activity and is structured to grow over time.

Can I buy products on this website?

Not yet. The website is currently under development and does not process orders or payments. Store, Catalog and Offers will become available as each area launches.

Are the images in Explorations real products?

No. They are concept visuals that illustrate the areas the company is exploring. They do not represent current products, services, prices or availability.

Are legal requirements the same everywhere?

No. Laws can differ between Puerto Rico, U.S. states, territories and local jurisdictions. A product or service that is lawful in Puerto Rico is not necessarily lawful everywhere.

When sales begin, some products may not be offered or shipped to certain destinations. Customers are responsible for making sure that what they order is lawful where they live and where it will be delivered.

Do I always need to provide a license or ID?

No. Standard orders are generally expected to require only the information needed to complete the purchase, unless applicable law or our internal verification criteria require additional information.

Documentation may be needed in two different situations:

  • When applicable law requires it for a particular transaction. In that case it is mandatory.
  • When an order meets our internal risk criteria. In that case the request is a company policy used for fraud prevention and order review. It does not mean the law requires it.
When can additional verification be requested?

Additional verification may be requested when it is required by applicable law, or when an order meets the company's internal risk criteria, for example:

  • wholesale or high-volume orders, such as approximately 8 or more units in a single transaction;
  • orders of unusually high value;
  • high-value customization services;
  • unusual ordering or payment patterns;
  • signs of possible fraud or identity misuse;
  • billing and shipping information that does not match or cannot be confirmed;
  • other circumstances that reasonably require a manual review.
What happens with wholesale or high-volume orders?

Orders that are commercial in nature, or that reach our internal quantity threshold, are classified by the company as wholesale or high-volume. This is a business classification, not a legal one.

These orders are placed in manual review before they are processed. We may ask for business information, identity verification or other reasonable documentation. Once the review is complete, the order is either processed or cancelled, and any payment is refunded.

Does the 8-unit threshold come from the law?

No. The threshold of approximately 8 or more units is an internal business risk-control criterion set by 2099 Industries. It is not a quantity established by Puerto Rico law or by any other law, and the company may adjust it over time.

Why might my order require manual review?

An order may be marked as “Pending Verification” when it meets one of our internal risk criteria, when applicable law requires verification, or when some information cannot be confirmed automatically. We will contact you by email if anything else is needed. Manual review protects customers and the company against fraud; it is not an accusation.

How can I contact 2099 Industries?

For quotes and orders as an individual, write to sales@2099ind.com. Companies and organizations can write to business@2099ind.com. For questions, claims, special requests or anything that needs personal attention, write to support@2099ind.com.
