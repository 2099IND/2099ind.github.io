Accessibility

Este documento está disponible solo en inglés. La versión en inglés es la que rige.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

2099 Industries wants this website to be usable by everyone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a visible focus indicator and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Text and interface contrast that meets WCAG AA.
  • Descriptive text alternatives for images; decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Respect for the “reduce motion” setting: animations are switched off when it is enabled.
  • Content that remains available without JavaScript and when zoomed up to 400%.
  • Touch targets of at least 44 × 44 pixels.
Known limitations

The INDEX navigation menu requires a recent browser. In older browsers it is hidden, and every section remains reachable by scrolling.

Feedback

If you find a barrier on this website, please tell us at support@2099ind.com and include the page and a short description. We will do our best to respond and fix it.
