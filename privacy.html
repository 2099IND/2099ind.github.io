Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information 2099 Industries collects, why it is collected and how it is protected. We collect only what is reasonably necessary.

What information do we collect today?

This website is informational. At present we collect only:

  • Emails you send us. If you write to one of our addresses, we receive your email address and the content of your message.
  • Basic technical data held by our hosting provider. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security and operational purposes under its own privacy statement.

We do not currently collect identification documents, licenses or payment information.

Do we use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers?

No. This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If that changes, this policy will be updated before any such tool is enabled.

Information we may collect when sales begin

When orders become available, we may collect the information needed to complete and support a purchase, such as name, contact details, shipping and billing addresses and order details. Payment card details are expected to be handled by a third-party payment processor rather than stored by us. This policy will be updated before sales begin.

Verification information

If an order requires verification, either because applicable law requires it or because the order meets our internal risk criteria, we may request information such as a government-issued ID, a license where appropriate, or business information for wholesale orders. Verification will follow these principles:

  • We request only the information reasonably necessary for the specific verification purpose.
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel.
  • Where possible, we keep a record that verification took place rather than unnecessary copies of sensitive documents.
  • Verification documents are not retained indefinitely.
How do we use your information?
  • To reply to your messages and provide customer support.
  • To keep the website secure and working.
  • When sales begin: to process and deliver orders, verify orders, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.
  • For other legitimate business purposes that are consistent with this policy.
Do we sell or share your personal information?

No. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example, email, hosting and, in the future, payment providers), or when required by law.

How long do we keep information?

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law. Specific retention periods will be defined according to the company's operational and legal requirements, and this policy will be updated to reflect them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by writing to support@2099ind.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; we will honor them as required.

Children

This website is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and revise the date shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@2099ind.com.
